---
title: "Surfing"
date: 2026-10-25
---

As I grow older, I find myself increasingly wondering about the moments in my life when I was truly happy.

Buddhist philosophy teaches us that happiness, or rather, harmony, is found here and now. Always, in the simplest of moments. I really appreciate the essence of these reflections and the ideas behind them, but I must admit that I've always had a tendency to seek out life experiences and circumstances that fill life with vivid colors.

Sometimes I manage to do so more successfully than others. But when I first tried standing on a board in 2021, I immediately realized that this would become my absolute passion. I would be consumed by it, irreversibly. And even during periods spent away from the ocean, my heart would keep longing to return, again and again.
